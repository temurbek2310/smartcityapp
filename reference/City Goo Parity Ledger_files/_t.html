CRASH src/data/home-content.ts

Home threw on every load before this audit

The uncommitted extraction of home-content.ts uses Hospital, ShoppingBag and History as category and service icons without importing them. ReferenceError: Hospital is not defined fired during module evaluation, so /home rendered the error boundary — "Ekranni ochib bo'lmadi" — and nothing else.

Fixed during this audit by adding the three imports. Seven unused-import warnings (Bike, Bus, Clock3, Compass, Landmark, Stethoscope, Users) are left over from the same extraction and are still there — left alone, since that refactor is yours and in flight.

Worth noting: the error boundary your notes say was never runtime-triggered has now been triggered, and it caught this correctly.

Renders a magenta gradient hero, a description, two tiles (hours, hourly price), an English amenity list — CCTV, Covered, Valet, Accessible, Car Wash — and Navigate / Reserve buttons. The reference's pkDetail() is a different document entirely:

  • Тарифы — hour / day / month grid, plate-recognition vs ticket badge, the free-first-15-minutes rule
  • Схема мест — level chips, a 6-wide bay grid with a drive-aisle divider, five bay states (free, busy, EV, accessible, yours) with a legend, tap to pick your bay
  • Rating with review count, street address, opening hours and height clearance
  • Amenity grid with counts — "EV · 6 шт.", "МГН · 4 места" — not a flat list

SETTLED
  • App is in Uzbek, reference in Russian — parity governs structure and visuals, not copy
  • No phone frame or fake status bar, because B SPACE is a real installable PWA
  • Real MapLibre + OpenStreetMap tiles instead of the hand-drawn Tashkent SVG — and so no tile-picker, no Google Maps API-key sheet, no map diagnostics panel
  • Forecasts generated from a per-lot peakProfile enum rather than 56 hand-written percentages
  • live-availability.ts as a pure function of (id, tick) rather than mutating the catalog
  • Profile statistics computed from real reservations, not a hardcoded "128 trips"
  • Occupancy band moved off the parking map into a chip row beside the results
  • Bookings share uses the real clipboard; Privacy exports the real state blob — both were toasts in the reference

Where this leaves the rebuild

Five of nine screens are genuinely done. The remaining work isn't spread evenly — it concentrates in three places: the parking detail screen (which has its data model already built and waiting), the missing full-screen map, and Home's palette, which is the one place the app visibly contradicts its own design system.

Everything above was verified against the running app on localhost:3000 and the reference served side by side; none of it is inferred from source alone.
